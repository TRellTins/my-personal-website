Mini FAQ

How long is the quiz?
About 3–5 minutes.
What happens after?
You’ll see a short summary and suggested next steps. You can book a consult if you want support.
Do I have to start coaching?
No. The quiz stands on its own.
Is this therapy?
No. This is practical, present focused behavioral coaching. That is action driven and non judgmental.
How much does a habit coach / behavioral coaching cost?
$45 for 25 minutes , $75 for 50 minutes and $135 for 90 minutes. You pay at booking. Refunds are available up to 24 hours before your session.
Why are there different session lengths (25, 50, 90 minutes)?
Different lengths fit different needs and energy levels. 25 minutes is a focused check-in to keep momentum. 50 minutes is a standard working session for planning and problem solving. 90 minutes are for deeper work when you want space to map systems, review patterns, or reset routines without rushing.
What happens after?
I review your responses, identify the best-fit session length (25/50/90 minutes) and focus, and share 1–3 clear next steps.
Will I get a copy of my quiz results?
Yes—on request. Provide your email at the end and I’ll send a copy of your responses along with tailored next steps.
Is this virtual or in-person?
Both. I serve Mechanicsville/Richmond locally and coach online for clients nationwide.
How often are sessions?
Weekly works best for momentum. We can adjust cadence based on your capacity and goals.
What if I miss a session?
You can reschedule up to 12 hours before. Within 24 hours there are no refunds, but we’ll get you back on track.
What if I’ve “failed” at habits before?
We design for your real week, not an ideal one. Small, sustainable moves beat willpower and “all-or-nothing.”
What does an accountability coach / behavioral coach do?
My job is to helps you break old patterns, build new habits, and stay consistent with your goals through tailored one-on-one support.
Is behavioral change coaching effective?
Yes—coaching is designed to help you bridge the gap between intention and action, delivering practical support and real results for anyone ready to change.
