The Story Behind My Logo

Each symbol represents a quality you’ll build on your journey of real change.
Our Logo: The Power of Three

Right at the heart of my brand, you’ll find a logo with three icons side by side: the owl, the fox, and the phoenix. I chose these because real, lasting change takes more than just willpower—it takes wisdom, flexibility, and the ability to bounce back. These symbols are here to remind you: you’ve got what it takes, and you’re not in this alone.

Phoenix – Resilience

Setbacks are part of any journey, and that’s where the phoenix comes in. This symbol is all about getting back up, dusting yourself off, and finding strength in the lessons you learn along the way. Resilience isn’t about never falling down—it’s about always finding a way to rise again, even stronger than before.

Owl – Wisdom

Every change starts with a bit of honest self-reflection. The owl is all about tuning in, asking yourself the tough questions, and being open to what you find—even if it’s uncomfortable. You don’t need to have all the answers; you just need to be willing to notice your patterns and learn as you go.

Fox – Adaptability

Let’s be real: progress is rarely a straight line. The fox reminds us that it’s okay to switch things up, try a new approach, or pivot when something isn’t working. Adaptability is about staying open, getting creative, and giving yourself permission to keep experimenting until you find what fits.

Both. Most clients work with me virtually, but in-person is available in Mechanicsville, VA.

Everything is tailored—no cookie-cutter plans. I focus on your goals, habits, and readiness for change.
